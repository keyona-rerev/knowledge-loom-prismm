# Insight Forge - Client Guide

Plain-language guide for the person whose business this install serves. One action per step. Words in **bold** are button and page names you will see on screen.

---

## 1. What this system does

Insight Forge reads material about your business - your website, your show transcripts, articles you save - and writes draft posts for your LinkedIn page. You approve every draft yourself. Nothing posts on its own.

The weekly rhythm:

1. The system prepares draft posts for you.
2. You open the drafts and approve the ones you like.
3. Approved posts publish to your LinkedIn page on the schedule.

---

## 2. Open your app

1. Open your browser.
2. Go to your app address (your installer gives you this - it ends in `github.io`).
3. Log in with your email and password.

You land on the **Dashboard**. Every section is one card. Click a card to open it. Use your browser's back button to return.

---

## 3. Review and approve a draft (the step you will use most)

1. On the **Dashboard**, click the **Review** card.
2. You see a list of drafts waiting for you.
3. Click a draft to read it in full.
4. If you like it, click the ✅ **approve** mark.
5. If you want changes, click **Revise** and type what you want different, in plain words. Example: "shorter, and mention the radio show." The system rewrites the draft and it comes back to Review.
6. If you do not want a draft at all, click **Reject**.

Approved drafts move to your **Calendar** and publish on their scheduled day. You never need to touch LinkedIn yourself.

---

## 4. Check what is scheduled

1. On the **Dashboard**, click the **Calendar** card.
2. You see your approved posts on their dates.
3. To move a post, open it and pick a new date.

---

## 5. Ask for a change to how the system writes

Small change to one post: use **Revise** on that draft (step 3).

Lasting change - tone, length, topics to avoid: tell your installer in plain words. They update the settings page, which is the standing written record of how your system runs: the model it uses, your posting schedule, and every preference. Months can pass between changes and nothing is lost, because it is written there.

---

## 6. What this costs

Your installer keeps a current cost table with each price and its source, checked on the day it was written. The pieces:

- **App hosting and database**: free tiers exist at the providers used. One real caveat: the free database pauses after a week with no activity. If that happens, the fix is logging into the database provider and pressing restore - a one-minute fix your installer can also do.
- **The AI**: runs under an account set up for your install. Your installer tells you which plan yours uses and what it costs per month.
- **Sending your posts to LinkedIn**: the connector has a free allowance for the first accounts.

Prices change. The table in your install materials has the date it was checked.

---

## 7. If something stops working

In order:

1. **No new drafts this week**: tell your installer. One possible cause is the paused free database (above), a one-minute fix.
2. **A post did not publish**: open **Calendar** and check the post is still there, then tell your installer.
3. **You cannot log in**: use the password-reset link on the login page, then tell your installer if it still fails.

You cannot break anything by looking. Every screen is safe to open.

---

## 8. What the system never does

- It never posts without your ✅.
- It never asks you for passwords or keys by email. Your login is the only password you need, and you type it only into your own app.
- It does not read your personal email.
